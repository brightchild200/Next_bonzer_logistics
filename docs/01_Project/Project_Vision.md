# Project Vision

## Vision

Build a modern, scalable, and production-ready ERP platform for logistics businesses that replaces fragmented operational processes with a unified digital workflow.

The ERP should simplify customer acquisition, enquiry management, pricing, quotations, operations, and job execution while remaining flexible enough to support future expansion.

---

# Goals

- Centralize business operations
- Eliminate duplicate data
- Improve operational efficiency
- Standardize business workflows
- Provide role-based access control
- Enable future automation
- Support enterprise scalability

---

# Core Objectives

- Secure Authentication

- Customer Management
- CRM
- Sales Pipeline
- Pricing Management
- Quotation Management
- Operations
- Job Management
- Reporting & Analytics

---

# Design Philosophy

- Simplicity
- Maintainability
- Scalability
- Security
- Consistency
- Modularity

---

# Development Philosophy

Every business workflow is finalized before implementation.

Architecture drives implementation—not the other way around.

No feature is implemented until:

- Workflow is approved
- Architecture is finalized
- Database design is reviewed
- Permissions are defined
