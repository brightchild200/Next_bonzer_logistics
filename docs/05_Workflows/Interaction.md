# Customer Interaction Workflow

## Status

✅ Frozen

---

# Purpose

A Customer Interaction represents the beginning of a conversation between the company and a customer.

It acts as the parent record for all future follow-ups.

---

# Workflow

Customer

↓

Create Interaction

↓

Multiple Follow-ups

↓

Optional Enquiry

---

# Supported Interaction Types

Examples include:

- Phone Call
- Meeting
- Email
- WhatsApp
- Office Visit

---

# Business Rules

- One interaction represents one conversation.
- Follow-ups extend the same interaction.
- Do not create a new interaction for every customer communication.
- Multiple follow-ups may exist under a single interaction.
- An enquiry may be created from an interaction when appropriate.

---

# Responsibilities

Salesperson

- Create interaction
- Record discussion
- Schedule follow-up
- Update interaction

Sales Manager

- Monitor interactions
- Review customer activity

Admin

- Full visibility

---

# Outcome

The interaction becomes the root record for future customer communication.
